**Purpose of this file:** Preserve the reasoning, order of operations, and current application sprint without duplicating every status field from Notion.

## Search thesis

Prioritize roles at the intersection of:

- AI-native product development.
- Designing directly in production code.
- Product ownership under ambiguity.
- Human-agent interaction, trust, evaluation, or developer workflows.
- React and TypeScript product surfaces where design judgment is an advantage.

Do not prioritize a company merely because it is prominent in AI. The role must turn Chris's design, founder, product, and engineering range into an advantage rather than asking the employer to overlook it.

## Operating rules

1. Protect active interview processes before starting new cold applications.
2. Finish one high-quality application before opening another.
3. Treat location requirements as real constraints; do not answer an application dishonestly.
4. Prefer warm follow-ups and referrals over cold applications when available.
5. Tailor the first summary, skills, and first two experience bullets to the role; do not rewrite career history from scratch.
6. Record application status, contact, dates, and follow-ups only in Notion.
7. Reuse each completed application as test data for the resume compiler.
8. Before submitting, run a six-to-eight-second review of only the summary, most recent role, first two bullets, and skills; the intended role must be unmistakable.
9. Mirror exact job-description language only when an approved accomplishment supports it.
10. Require lead bullets to show ownership, relevant skill or method, measurement, and impact in language a non-technical recruiter can follow.

## Resume compiler timing

Start the compiler soon, but not before the current PostHog submission or at the cost of active interviews.

### Minimum viable compiler sprint

After PostHog and the first two applications in the next sprint, spend one or two focused half-days on the smallest useful compiler:

1. Create canonical, approved facts for y30 and PlaySesh.
2. Add three profiles: `ai-product-designer`, `design-engineer`, and `ai-product-engineer`.
3. Use PostHog, Runway, and LiveKit as the first job-description fixtures.
4. Produce selected content JSON and `review.md` before attempting PDF design.
5. Verify that the same facts produce meaningfully different summaries, skills, and first bullets.
6. Continue applying while the rendering layer is built; do not wait for the complete system.

### When the compiler becomes the default

Use the compiler for live applications only after it can:

- Trace every rendered claim to an approved fact.
- Preserve dates, metrics, qualifiers, and attribution.
- Produce a role-specific content model that Chris approves.
- Generate selectable PDF text with acceptable page count and reading order.
- Match or exceed the visual quality bar documented in `RESUME_COMPILER_PLAN.md`.

Until then, manually tailor only the minimum necessary fields and feed approved improvements back into the fact model.

3. Did any application reveal a reusable fact or phrasing for the compiler?
4. Are applications being delayed by tooling work?
5. Is the role mix still centered on AI-native design-by-code work?
