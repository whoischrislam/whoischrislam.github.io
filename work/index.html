Case studies

The work, in full

Seven case studies across healthcare, learning platforms, and marketplaces. Each one says what shipped, what it moved, and where I was wrong.

Hiring for healthcare or a high-trust product? Start with Clover Care Visit, then Iodine.

Hiring for a complex platform with real users mid-flight? Start with Pathstream. It is the deepest one here.

Hiring for marketplace or consumer? Start with TaskRabbit.

Hiring for data-heavy or editorial work? Start with GoodRx Research.

Pathstream

Rebuilding the lesson, mid-flight →
Senior Product Design Manager, operating as lead designer. First design hire. 2019–2020.

Rebuilt the core lesson experience of a live learning platform while students were mid-course, without forcing a rewrite of 60–80 hours of existing course content. Also the Labs PRD, across students, instructors, and course authors. I never measured whether the redesign worked, and the case study says so.

Iodine

Helping people make better decisions about prescription drugs →
Lead Product Designer, first full-time designer. 2017–2018.

A redesigned coupon flow moved conversion from 2% to 5%. Drug-alternative pages nearly doubled session time. Iodine.com won the Webby for Best Health Website in 2018.

GoodRx Research

Making prescription drug prices legible to the people paying them →
Lead Product Designer, research and experimentation team. 2017–2018.

Conversion work across the drug pages: 10% lift on mobile, 25% on desktop. Data visualisations referenced in the New York Times and on Good Morning America. Fifty-state price fact sheets built with co-CEO Doug Hirsch, used in advocacy around S.2554 and S.2553.

Clover Health — Care Visit

Empowering nurse practitioners to better care for members →
Designer, operating as product design lead. Second designer. 2015–2016.

Rebuilt the nurse-practitioner home-visit app after a week in New Jersey shadowing live visits. About 7–8× ROI on visit-related revenue, and NPs stopped losing one to two hours every night to paper forms.

Clover Health — the website

Clover Health's first website →
Designer. 2015–2016.

Shipped ahead of 2016 enrollment, serving prospective members, existing members, brokers, and providers from one site. The identity carried through to billboards, bus decals, and information stands.

TaskRabbit — Tasker experience

Empowering Taskers to deliver a better customer experience →
Product Designer, owned the Tasker side. 2014–2015.

Tasker NPS recovered from about −5 in mid-2014 to the mid-50s. Client NPS to the high-40s. TaskRabbit was later acquired by IKEA.

TaskRabbit — Client experience

TaskRabbit was rebuilding itself from the ground up →
Product Designer. Hired by founder and CEO Leah Busque. 2014–2015.

The move from bidding to direct hire meant clients could finally see who they were working with, what it would cost, and when it would be done. The old model answered none of those.
